# Open Questions for Stage 2

## Questions for Jaguar Land Rover

1. When did JLR first identify abnormal crankshaft/main-bearing failure within the 3.0 TDV6/SDV6 family?
2. What root cause or causes were identified for SSM71816, SSM72578 and any superseding communications?
3. Why was maximum main-bearing crankshaft run-out tolerance reduced from 50 μm to 30 μm?
4. Which engine serial numbers and vehicle production ranges contain the earlier specification?
5. What later changes were made to crankshafts, bearing shells, bearing retention, oilways, oil pumps, blocks, bedplates or assembly procedures?
6. What engineering changes define the “improved engine” used in the Chinese recall remedy?
7. Are those changes incorporated in UK service/remanufactured engines?
8. What is the exact internal specification of replacement engine LR127427 and its supersessions?
9. How many original 3.0 TDV6/SDV6 engines have been replaced in the UK?
10. How many JLR replacement/remanufactured engines subsequently failed?
11. What were the most common failure diagnoses and mileages?
12. When did JLR cease supplying newly manufactured complete engine assemblies for relevant applications?
13. Who remanufactures the current service engines and what components are reused/replaced?
14. What failure-rate comparison exists between original and remanufactured engines?
15. What information concerning China and South Korea was supplied to DVSA?
16. What technical basis was used to determine whether UK vehicles required equivalent recall action?

## Questions for DVSA

1. When did DVSA first receive complaints concerning 3.0 TDV6/SDV6 crankshaft or main-bearing failure?
2. Did DVSA open a defect investigation? If so, what was its reference and conclusion?
3. Did DVSA obtain SSM71816, SSM72578 or equivalent JLR technical information?
4. Did DVSA inspect or independently examine failed engines?
5. What manufacturer warranty, field-failure or replacement-engine data did DVSA receive?
6. Was the Chinese 68,828-vehicle recall reviewed for UK relevance?
7. Was the South Korean recall/re-recall reviewed for UK relevance?
8. Did JLR provide a written explanation why equivalent UK recall action was not undertaken?
9. Were UK production/VIN populations compared with overseas recalled vehicles?
10. What records within the 600+ potentially relevant documents specifically concern crankshaft, main-bearing, bearing-shell rotation, seizure, metallic debris or sudden loss of motive power?
11. Did DVSA consider whether sudden engine seizure meets the UK safety-defect threshold?
12. Were records retained of any investigation concluded not to be safety-related?
